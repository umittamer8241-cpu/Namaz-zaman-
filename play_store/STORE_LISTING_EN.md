# Google Play — Namaz Zamanı (EN)

**Short description:**
Daily prayer times, Qibla, and a rotating verse and hadith for your location.

**Full description:**
Namaz Zamanı displays daily prayer times and the direction of the Qibla on one clear screen.

• Fajr, sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha
• Countdown to the next prayer
• Automatic location through Google location services, with GPS fallback
• Qibla compass on supported phones
• Phone and ship time-zone options
• Interface and daily wisdom in 12 languages, including English, Arabic, Turkish and Russian
• A themed verse excerpt and a related hadith meaning summary that change every day
• Offline astronomical time calculation and offline daily content

**Important:** Prayer times are approximate astronomical calculations and may differ from local official schedules. Check your location and selected time zone. Ship Wi-Fi alone may not provide the real location at sea. Metal structures may affect compass accuracy.

Verse/hadith meanings are brief summaries with references, not official verbatim translations.
